# Test checklist (Pixel 8)

## On the phone
- Boot, setup wizard, Google sign-in
- Face unlock (enrol + unlock), fingerprint
- SIM: mobile data, 5G, VoLTE/VoNR calls, SMS, Wi-Fi calling
- Audio routes: speaker, earpiece, wired, Bluetooth; JamesDSP on/off
- Camera: photo, every video mode, front camera
- NFC, GPS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, charging, display, vibration
- Now Playing, Circle to Search (long-press the pill), Gemini
- ROM extras: PowerInsight, Datura firewall (block an app and unblock it), blur slider, pill toggle, black theme surfaces

## From logs (bugreport after ~10 min of use)
- No crashes, ANRs, tombstones or crash loops
- No new SELinux denials from vendor HALs
- No modem resets; IMS registered
